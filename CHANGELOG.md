# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release: 129 checkpoints (38 τ²-bench, 37 BFCL, 54 AppWorld, AppWorld contents not distributed) and 24 retail and airline
checkpoints; the evaluation harness (restore and replay verification, τ²-bench and BFCL runners, stage-1 scoring including the retail
and airline per-write policy rules, the semantic review of stage 2 with the pinned judge's rubrics, and the acceptance rule); every
continuation of the paper; scripts that reproduce every table and Figure 2.
